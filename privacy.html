Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Post Everywhere is a desktop application designed to help users publish video content to supported social media platforms.

Information We Access

Post Everywhere may access information authorized by the user through third-party platform APIs, including account identification information and permissions necessary to upload or publish content.

Video and Content Data

Videos, titles, captions, and other content selected by the user are processed only for the purpose of sending that content to platforms selected by the user.

Authentication

Authentication is performed using authorization systems provided by the relevant third-party platforms. Post Everywhere does not request or store users' social media passwords.

Data Sharing

Post Everywhere does not sell personal information. Information and content are transmitted only to services selected and authorized by the user as necessary to provide the application's publishing functionality.

Data Retention

Post Everywhere is primarily a locally operated desktop application. Authentication credentials or tokens required for connected services may be stored locally on the user's computer.

Third-Party Services

Use of connected platforms is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective platforms.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of Post Everywhere.
